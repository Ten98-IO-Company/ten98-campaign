# Ten98 Campaign

Plan, publish and measure your campaigns across social, advertising, out-of-home, email, SMS and WhatsApp from one self-hosted workspace.

Campaign is an open-source campaign manager for marketing teams who want one place to run social publishing, online ads, out-of-home media, direct messaging, digital assets and campaign planning, on their own server. Workspaces keep each team's projects, people and provider connections separate.

![Campaign ads manager](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/Ten98-IO-Company/ten98-campaign/images/ads-verification.png)

## Why Campaign

- **One composer for every channel.** Write once, then tailor captions, media and options for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, X, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky, Reddit, Google Business Profile, Telegram, Discord and Slack. Each copy is checked before it is published or scheduled.
- **Ads without surprises.** Create and manage ads on Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, X, Reddit and ChatGPT, plus native Google Shopping and Microsoft Search campaigns. New ads start paused, and uncertain provider results are never silently repeated.
- **Out-of-home media.** Find Broadsign screens, see them on a map, save proposals and book them with explicit confirmation.
- **Email, SMS and WhatsApp campaigns.** Transactional email, two-way SMS and approved WhatsApp templates, with consent records, contact clean-up, suppression and opt-out handling.
- **One inbox.** Messages, comments, reviews and SMS replies from your connected accounts, with team triage.
- **Links and tracking.** Short links, QR codes and cookie-free website tracking that stores no visitor identifiers.
- **Approvals that mean something.** Optional reviews for posts and ad spend; approval never publishes or spends on its own.
- **Assets and planning.** A Google Drive asset library, a publishing calendar with optional Google Calendar sync, and optional AI drafts through OpenRouter that are saved only after you review them.
- Self-hosted and open source under the MIT licence.
