# Zernio coverage: stores, administration and additional channels

Snapshot: 18 October 2026, compared with the v0.8.7 expansion and Zernio OpenAPI 1.292.0. This is an implementation backlog, not a claim that the missing features work. The [main breakdown](zernio-feature-plan.md) defines status and acceptance rules; the [operation inventory](zernio-api-inventory.csv) lists the API surface without treating every operation as a separate user feature.

Blog and commerce support is partial. Scoped blog/article reads, reviewed drafts/publication and WordPress scheduling/published-content editing have bounded synthetic-provider acceptance; Shopify published-content editing remains contract-blocked. Shopify products support scoped reads, reviewed title/HTML and single-variant price edits, draft creation and state controls, plus read-only inventory/location and collection workflows. Archived-product deletion and repaired receipt-only checks passed the separate frozen994 automated and bounded browser gates. Use [canonical verification](verification.md#current-accepted-checkpoint) for each frozen source generation, test log and browser limit; historical receipts are not new evidence. Restoration, sales-channel controls, broader product fields and complete commerce parity remain unfinished.

## Stores and articles

[Connection setup](../src/ZernioConnection.tsx) and [scoped handlers](../server/zernio-connections.ts) provide Shopify and WordPress onboarding. [Blog UI](../src/ZernioBlogDrafts.tsx) and [blog handlers](../server/zernio-blogs.ts) provide browsing, article reads, reviewed DRAFT creation/editing, publication changes, WordPress scheduling and reviewed WordPress-only published-content editing. [Shopify product UI](../src/ZernioProducts.tsx) and [Commerce handler](../server/zernio-products.ts) add store-capability, filtered product-page, detail and stock/location reads plus reviewed title/HTML editing, single-variant price editing, draft creation, active-to-draft deactivation, collection list/detail, explicit collection-filtered product reads and reviewed title-only collection renaming. A new Archive candidate reuses that reviewed product-state flow within the separate boundary below. Article/product management remains incomplete.

## Provider administration and diagnostics

Local application users, invitations, roles and OIDC already exist. They authorize access to this app; they are not Zernio team membership or Zernio enterprise SSO. New provider administration belongs to instance operators with workspace administration, not ordinary campaign editors.

| Feature family | Current app | Required workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Provider identity and members | Candidate — read-only member inspection; combined acceptance pending | Explicit member reads require an instance operator who is also a workspace owner/admin, with fresh session, membership, credential and mapping checks. Only allowlisted native member fields are returned, with one distinct provider caller. Full-access credentials are the documented baseline; upstream denials remain unavailable. No provider team name/ID, credential scope or local membership grant is inferred. [Users](https://docs.zernio.com/users/list-users), [focused checks](../tests/zernio-admin.test.mjs) |
| Zernio team invitations | Missing | Separate provider invite flow with explicit permission impact; keep existing local invitations unchanged. [Provider invites](https://docs.zernio.com/invites/create-invite-token) |

Acceptance: operator versus editor/viewer access; full versus restricted/profile-scoped keys; revoked credentials; pagination; safe secret display; usage unavailable/zero states; and support-run retries without a second charge. Provider grants or revocations must be deliberate user actions.

A fallback cannot silently turn one intended message into two sends.
